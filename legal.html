Like every website, the server that delivers these pages keeps ordinary technical logs — the internet address a request came from, the time, which file was requested, and what browser asked for it. Those logs are used to keep the site running and secure, are kept only briefly by the host, and are not used by McLearn Media to build a profile of you or to market to you.

Cookies

We set none. Your browser may store the audio files temporarily so a snippet does not have to download twice; that is your browser's own cache, under your control, and it never reaches us.

Fonts

Typefaces on this page load from Google Fonts. When they load, Google receives the request and can see the internet address it came from, in the same way any external file request works. We send Google nothing about you beyond that request. If you would rather not have that happen, a browser that blocks third-party requests will simply show the page in a default typeface, and everything still works.

The email and phone links on this site open your own email app or dialer. Nothing is sent until you send it. If you do write or call, we keep that correspondence so we can answer you, and we do not sell it, rent it, trade it, or add you to any list.
