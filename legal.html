Privacy & Terms

McLearn Media LLC · Brandon, Mississippi

This page explains what this website does with information, and the terms you agree to by using it. It is written in plain language on purpose. If something here is unclear, call or write and ask.

Privacy notice

The short version. You can listen to every song on this site without telling us who you are. We only keep what you choose to give us on the MP fan page and in the Reading Room: a name and message you post on the fan wall, or an email address you leave to hear about new music and books. We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and we never sell, rent or trade anything you give us.

What we collect, and why

Fan wall posts. When you post on the fan wall, we keep the name you type, your message and the time you posted. Your post is public: anyone who visits the page can read it. Don't put anything in a post you wouldn't want strangers to see, such as your phone number, address or email.

Email sign-ups. When you join the email list, from the MP fan page or the Reading Room, we keep your email address, the date you signed up and which page you joined from. We use it only to send you news about MP's music, the McLearn Media books and other McLearn Media releases. Every email we send will tell you how to leave the list.

Fan votes. When you vote on what MP shares next, your phone or computer keeps a random number so you can change your vote later. We store a scrambled (hashed) version of that number with your choice. It isn't linked to your name, your email or anything else about you. Only the totals are shown.

Dedication cards. Cards are made on your own device and we don't keep them. If you choose to post one on the fan wall, it's treated like any other fan wall post.

Spam protection. To stop the same person posting over and over, we keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your internet address for about one minute after you post, sign up or vote. It can't be turned back into your address, and it deletes itself.

That's everything. Listening to songs, opening the full-song links and reading the page don't send us any information about you.

Where it's kept

Fan wall posts and the email list are stored with Cloudflare, the company that hosts this site, on servers it runs for us. Only McLearn Media can see the email list. We don't share it with anyone, except where the law requires it.

What our host records

Like every website, the server that delivers these pages keeps ordinary technical logs — the internet address a request came from, the time, which file was requested, and what browser asked for it. That happens automatically, at the hosting layer, and is standard for all web traffic. Those logs are used to keep the site running and secure, are kept only briefly by the host, and are not used by McLearn Media to build a profile of you or to market to you.

Cookies and browser storage

We set no cookies. If you post on the fan wall, your browser remembers the name you typed so you don't have to type it again, and the random number used for your fan vote is kept the same way; both stay on your own device and you can clear it in your browser settings. Your browser may also keep a copy of the audio so a song doesn't have to download twice.

Fonts

Typefaces on this page load from Google Fonts. When they load, Google receives the request and can see the internet address it came from, in the same way any external file request works. We send Google nothing about you beyond that request. If you would rather not have that happen, a browser that blocks third-party requests will simply show the page in a default typeface, and everything still works.

When you contact us

The email and phone links on this site open your own email app or dialer. Nothing is sent until you send it. If you do write or call, we keep that correspondence so we can answer you, and we do not sell it, rent it, or trade it.

Children

You must be 13 or older to post on the fan wall or join the email list. If you are younger than 13, please don't post or sign up. If we learn that someone under 13 has given us information, we delete it.

Fan wall rules

Keep it kind. Links aren't allowed. McLearn Media may remove any post at any time, including anything hateful, threatening, sexual, spammy, or that shares someone's private information. By posting, you confirm the words are your own and give McLearn Media permission to show them on this site.

Your choices

You can leave the email list at any time, using the link in any email we send or the Leave the list button on the fan page. To have a fan wall post removed, or to ask what we hold about you, write to Mclearn2025@gmail.com and it will be handled.

Changes

If we change what this site collects, this notice will be updated before the change goes live, not after. Last updated October 2026.
